For preschoolers, pupils and students

New here? This is not a search engine but a free catalog of verified links to original sources.

Free, verified resources by age: learning sites for little ones, lessons and practice for schoolchildren, open courses for students, free textbooks, interactive models, educational games, cartoons and video channels. All links open in a new tab.

Age:

For preschoolers: ages 3–7

  • PBS KIDSUSA3–7

    Games and videos from US public television: letters, numbers, nature. Free, in English.

  • Khan Academy KidsUSA3–7

    A free, ad-free app for ages 2–8: reading, numbers, logic, creativity.

  • Sesame StreetUSA3–7

    Letters, numbers and friendship in games, songs and videos.

  • CBeebiesUK3–7

    The BBC channel for under-sixes: games, songs and shows.

  • Peep and the Big Wide WorldUSA3–7

    First science and maths explorations: water, light, shadows, sound.

  • StoryWeaverIndia3–17

    Thousands of free illustrated children's books in hundreds of languages.

  • International Children's Digital LibraryUSA3–17

    Children's books from around the world, free to read online.

  • Aunt Owl's Lessons («Уроки тётушки Совы»)Russia3–7

    Alphabet, counting, safety and the world around us in short cartoons, in Russian.

For schoolchildren

  • InternetUrokRussia7–17

    Video lessons on the Russian school curriculum with notes and exercises, in Russian.

  • Khan AcademyUSA7+

    Free lessons and practice in maths, science and computing.

  • BBC BitesizeUK7–17

    Lessons, videos and quizzes for every school subject.

  • PhETUSA7+

    Interactive simulations in physics, chemistry, biology and maths from the University of Colorado, in dozens of languages.

  • GeoGebraAustria7+

    Free interactive maths: graphs, geometry and problems.

  • ScratchUSA7–17

    Coding for kids from MIT: build your own games and animations from blocks.

  • Code.orgUSA7–17

    Free courses in coding and computer science for schoolchildren.

  • NASA Space PlaceUSA7–17

    Space and Earth science for kids: experiments, games and explanations from NASA.

  • National Geographic KidsUSA7–17

    Animals, nature, countries and science facts for schoolchildren.

  • Sirius.Courses («Сириус.Курсы»)Russia7+

    The open online talent school of the Sirius centre: free courses for schoolchildren in maths, computer science and the natural sciences, olympiad preparation; in Russian.

  • TUMOArmenia7–17

    A free creative technology centre for teenagers aged 12–18: coding, design, animation, robotics.

  • ArmathArmenia7–17

    Engineering labs in Armenian schools: robotics and programming for schoolchildren.

  • Uchi.ruRussia7–17

    Interactive exercises in maths, Russian and other subjects, partly free; in Russian.

  • Khan Academy in ArmenianArmenia7–17

    Khan Academy lessons and practice in Armenian: maths and other subjects, free.

  • ImDproc («My School»)Armenia7–17

    An Armenian learning site for schoolchildren: exercises and tests by subject; many exercises need no subscription.

  • e-school ArmeniaArmenia7–17

    Distance learning for village schools in Armenia: video lessons in Armenian and mentoring.

  • HyeLearnArmenian diaspora3–17

    Interactive Western Armenian lessons for children aged 5–11: audio, culture, a Sunday-school curriculum; free for families.

  • LearnArmeniaArmenia7+

    Armenian online: the 39-letter alphabet, over 1,450 words and 770 phrases voiced by native speakers, 31 grammar topics; exercises are free, some games are paid.

  • CourseraUSA17+

  • Open Education («Открытое образование»)Russia17+

  • Erasmus+EU17+

  • arXivUSA17+

Interactive models and simulations

  • DesmosUSA7+

    An online graphing calculator: functions, equations and animated parameters; free, in many languages.

  • NASA EyesUSA7+

    The Solar System, exoplanets and spacecraft in real-time 3D, from NASA.

  • MolViewInternational7+

    Molecules in 3D: draw a formula or search for a substance and explore its structure.

  • Falstad Circuit SimulatorUSA7+

    Electric circuits in motion: build a circuit and watch the currents flow; free, in the browser.

  • Stellarium WebInternational3+

    The night sky in your browser for any place and time: constellations, planets, satellites.

  • Earth NullschoolInternational7+

    A live map of the Earth's winds, ocean currents and temperatures from weather forecast data.

  • PtableUSA7+

    An interactive periodic table: element properties, isotopes and compounds; in many languages.

  • PolypadUSA7–17

    A virtual maths kit: shapes, fractions, number tiles and probability; free.

  • oPhysicsUSA7+

    Interactive physics simulations: mechanics, waves, optics and electricity, for school and university; in English.

  • Seeing TheoryUSA17+

    A visual introduction to probability and statistics from Brown University.

  • ChemCollectiveUSA7+

    Virtual chemistry labs and problems from Carnegie Mellon University; in English.

  • Concord ConsortiumUSA7–17

    Free interactive models and lessons in science for schoolchildren; in English.

Educational games

  • LichessFrance7+

    Online chess: games, puzzles, analysis and lessons; completely free, no ads, open source, in many languages.

  • Chess.comUSA7+

    One of the largest chess sites: playing online is free, with puzzles and lessons; some lessons and analysis are paid.

  • ChessKidUSA7–17

    Chess for children from Chess.com: safe play, puzzles and lessons; playing is free, some lessons are paid.

  • Online Go Server (OGS)International7+

    Go, the ancient strategy game: games with people from all over the world, problems and a rules tutorial; free.

  • Blockly GamesInternational7–17

    First steps in programming: block puzzles such as maze, turtle and music; free, open source, in many languages.

  • NRICHUK3–17

    Maths games and problems from the University of Cambridge for children from age 3 to school leavers; free, in English.

  • SeterraSweden7+

    A geography trainer: countries, capitals, flags, rivers and mountains on the map; free, in many languages.

  • FolditUSA7+

    A science game: fold proteins and help researchers; created at the University of Washington; free, requires installing the program.

  • DuolingoUSA7+

    Language learning as a game: short daily lessons; the main part is free, a paid subscription is available.

Educational cartoons and films

  • «The Music Box with a Secret» (1976)USSR3–17

    A father and son find out why a music box went silent, and the viewer learns how the mechanism works. Based on Odoevsky's tale «The Town in a Snuffbox»; video in Russian.

  • «At the Back Desk» (1978–1985)USSR7–17

    A Soyuzmultfilm animated magazine of short stories about school and learning; in Russian.

  • «In the Land of Unlearned Lessons» (1969)USSR7–17

    A lazy pupil lands in a country where his mistakes come alive: why grammar and arithmetic matter; in Russian.

  • «The Island of Errors» (1955)USSR7–17

    A careless pupil's mistakes come alive on an island: crooked houses, wrong words and sums; in Russian.

  • Soyuzmultfilm — official channelRussia3–17

    The studio's cartoons, classic and new, free and legal.

  • «The Fixies» («Фиксики»)Russia3–17

    How the things around us work, from a battery to a computer.

  • «Kikoriki» («Смешарики»)Russia3–17

    Official site; the science series «Pin-Code» covers physics, chemistry, technology and space.

  • «Once Upon a Time… Life» and other «Il était une fois…» seriesFrance7–17

    The classic about how the human body works, plus series on history, space and inventors; official site in French.

  • NumberblocksUK3–7

    BBC cartoons about numbers: block characters add up and split.

  • AlphablocksUK3–7

    Letter blocks teach reading: sounds and words.
