Useful programs and apps

New here? This is not a search engine but a free catalog of verified links to original sources.

Verified free programs for computers and phones: learning, documents and books, creativity, internet and security, programming, help for teachers and people with special needs. Most are free and open source. Links go only to the developers’ official websites. Programs: 43.

Learning and knowledge

    Flashcards with spaced repetition for words, formulas and dates. Free on computers and Android; the iPhone app is paid.

  • GeoGebraWindowsmacOSLinuxAndroidiOS

    Maths apps: graphs, geometry, 3D and probability; they also work offline. Free.

  • GComprisWindowsmacOSLinuxAndroidopen source

    More than a hundred learning activities for children aged 2 to 10: counting, reading, logic and nature; in many languages.

  • Tux PaintWindowsmacOSLinuxAndroidopen source

    A simple drawing program for children: brushes, stamps and fun sounds.

  • KiwixWindowsmacOSLinuxAndroidiOSopen source

    Wikipedia and other reference works offline: download once and read anywhere.

  • ZoteroWindowsmacOSLinuxiOSopen source

    Collects sources for study and research and formats bibliographies in the style you need.

  • StellariumWindowsmacOSLinuxopen source
