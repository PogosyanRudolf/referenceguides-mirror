How to read reference data

Physical and chemical values are almost always published with their conditions: temperature, pressure, state of matter, measurement method. Compare data only under matching conditions.

When citing, name the publishing organisation (NIST, IUPAC, NOAA, NASA) and the year of the reference edition — a link to a website without a version is considered incomplete.
