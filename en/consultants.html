About the consultations

Consultations are independent expert help. The specialist does not make decisions for you and is not responsible for how you apply the conclusions.

The portal matches a consultant to your task: all consultations are run by independent subject specialists, with booking and scheduling via WhatsApp.

Medical, legal and engineering topics are discussed as work with sources and data, not as a substitute for a licensed professional.
